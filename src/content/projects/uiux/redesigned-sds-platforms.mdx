        >
        > Using ElevenLabs to convert the provided script into a voiceover.

        > ##### Pre-production
        >
        > Isolating and grouping interface elements in Figma; conceptualizing which parts to animate based on the brief and script.
        >
        > Transferring assets from Figma to Adobe XD to export them for Adobe After Effects.
        >
        > Organizing elements and creating folders for each section requiring animation; importing audio and music files; structuring the project to prepare for animation.
        {/* */}
        > ##### Production
        >
        > Creating the motion graphics based on the prepared sections, beats, and voiceover.
        >
        > Submitting progress updates (at the 30%, 50%, and 70% stages) and final versions to the relevant stakeholders.

<SectionHeading title="Key Takeaway" defaultExpanded />

<SectionContent>
    The biggest lesson from this project came from its timeline constraint.
    Because the motion video was prioritized, it had to be shipped using the
    **original**, **cluttered interface** instead of my **redesigned version**.
    {/* */}
    Seeing the final animation struggle to highlight key features on a messy
    dashboard made one thing clear to me. A successful product video requires
    **clean, simplified static frames** prepared long before any animation
    begins. This missed opportunity fueled my passion for creating
    **motion-ready UI**, ensuring that future animation teams have the perfect
    foundation to work with.
</SectionContent>
